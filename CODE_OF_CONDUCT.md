# Code of Conduct

NetOpsGuard welcomes constructive contributions from network engineers, administrators, security practitioners, and learners.

## Our expectations

Be respectful and professional. Discuss technical disagreements with evidence; criticize implementations rather than people. Do not harass, intimidate, discriminate against, or disclose private information about others. Respect network ownership and only share lab or device configurations that you are authorized to publish.

## Community discussions

Use issues and pull requests for relevant, civil discussion of NetOpsGuard. Do not post credentials, customer-sensitive information, personal attacks, spam, or exploit instructions.

If you experience misconduct, contact the repository maintainer through a private channel available on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, submit a public issue requesting private contact details **without describing private incidents or identities**.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove inappropriate community content and may limit participation to keep collaboration safe and useful.
