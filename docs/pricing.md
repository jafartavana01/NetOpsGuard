# NetOpsGuard pricing and network management value

**One platform for TACACS+ AAA, RADIUS, Active Directory/LDAP, configuration backup (NCM), Cisco security auditing and network operations.** NetOpsGuard Community is free for up to five devices. The paid editions primarily increase the permitted number of managed devices.

## Introductory annual licence prices

| Edition | Managed devices | Introductory annual licence |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Community | Up to 5 | **Free** |
| Starter | Up to 15 | **US$99/year** |
| Professional | Up to 50 | **US$299/year** |
| Business | Up to 100 | **US$599/year** |
| Enterprise | Up to 300 | **US$1,299/year** |
| Unlimited | No fixed device limit | **Contact for a quote** |

**USD, introductory rates.** These are proposed annual licence prices offered through the project maintainer, **subject to confirmation before purchase**; there is currently no checkout flow or guaranteed service-level agreement. Every edition includes the **currently available feature set**, subject to the installed daemon and device capabilities. The entitlement shown inside NetOpsGuard after licence import is authoritative.

## How does NetOpsGuard pricing compare with ManageEngine NCM?

ManageEngine offers **Network Configuration Manager (NCM)** as a dedicated configuration management product. Its Professional Edition annual subscription prices are publicly listed at US$238 for 10 devices, US$958 for 50 devices, and US$1,318 for 100 devices (2-user packages).

| Managed-device tier | NetOpsGuard introductory annual price | ManageEngine Network Configuration Manager Professional annual subscription |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 50 devices | **US$299** | **US$958** |
| 100 devices | **US$599** | **US$1,318** |

For the same **50-device** count, the published ManageEngine NCM price is **US$659 more per year** than the introductory NetOpsGuard Professional price. For **100 devices**, the difference is **US$719 per year**.

NetOpsGuard also makes TACACS+ device administration policies, RADIUS, AD/LDAP integration, security audits, and SSH command jobs available in its product. ManageEngine NCM provides dedicated network configuration functionality. This should be understood as **price and product-scope context**, not a claim that the two products have equivalent NCM coverage or support.

**Comparison note:** ManageEngine's prices are for its separate Network Configuration Manager **Professional Edition**, based on its published annual subscription store listing (checked **13 October 2026**, USD, 2-user packages). NetOpsGuard's prices are introductory proposed annual licence rates offered directly by the maintainer, subject to confirmation at ordering. This is a **pricing comparison, not a feature-equivalence or independent performance claim**: device/OS support, NCM functions, enterprise maturity, support terms and service levels differ. Vendor prices can change; check the [official ManageEngine store](https://store.manageengine.com/network-configuration-manager/index.html) before making a purchase decision.

## What is included in a NetOpsGuard licence?

- TACACS+ device administrator authentication, command authorization and accounting (compatible NAS required)
- Local and configured AD/LDAP identity and group integration
- RADIUS authentication/accounting when supported by the installed daemon and target device
- Device inventory and discovery
- NCM configuration archive, backup, comparison, drift and change views
- Cisco IOS/IOS-XE security auditing views, findings and reports
- Network Operations SSH command jobs and templates

Available functionality depends on the installed version, daemon build and device OS. In particular, verify **unattended NCM backup scheduling** in your actual environment rather than assuming a configured schedule has run. Per-command RADIUS authorization is not equivalent to TACACS+ command authorization. [Product limitations](https://github.com/jafartavana01/NetOpsGuard/blob/main/docs/CONFIGURATION.md).
